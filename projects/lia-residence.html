Lia Residence

Project Brief

Located on a unique D-shaped sea-facing plot, the design was inspired by the fluid nature of water and ocean waves.

As part of the design team working alongside the Senior Architects, I contributed to the development of a concept centered around movement and free-flowing forms, incorporating curves throughout the building while avoiding sharp corners.

Care was also taken to avoid overly symmetrical geometry, creating a more natural and dynamic architectural and interior expression.

Scope of Work
  • Architectural concept layout development
  • Calculations, e.g. NSA, BUA & GFA
  • Concept presentation development
  • Preliminary concept design submissions
  • Public area interior design development
