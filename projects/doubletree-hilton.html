Landscape Renovation — ExecutionJumeirah, Dubai

DoubleTree by Hilton Jumeirah

Project Brief

The project was undertaken from a design execution and contracting perspective. The scope included hardscape and softscape landscape works, adult and children's swimming pools integrated with a water curtain, changing rooms, a pool bar, an in-house kitchen, multiple kiosks, and walkway and seating pergolas.

The role involved coordinating with material suppliers and joinery teams, ensuring the execution matched the approved design, and adapting details to suit site conditions.

Scope of Work
  • Execution plans and sections development
  • Joinery package development
  • Tracking project timeline and deliverables
