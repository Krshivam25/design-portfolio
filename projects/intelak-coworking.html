Intelak Co-Working Space

Project Brief

The client had a clear vision for the project, including the color palette and key design requirements. Our role was to bring this vision to life while introducing innovative solutions and bespoke furniture designs. Most of the furniture was custom-made, including foldable tables and multifunctional podiums with integrated storage drawers.

The project was carried through from furniture design and detail drawings to project management and site supervision — and delivered as a fully built space.

Scope of Work
  • Furniture design
  • Material selection
  • Detail drawings for execution
  • Project management
  • Site supervision
